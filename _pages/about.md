Hi! I'm an incoming NSF Graduate Research Fellow and ARCS Scholar at the University of Washington, pursuing a PhD in Astronomy. My research interests lie at the intersection of galaxy formation and cosmology, with a particular focus on dark matter, its nature, and its influence on galaxy evolution.

I received my B.S. in Astrophysics from Yale University in 2025. During my undergraduate degree, I researched anisotropic satellite galaxy quenching, galaxy groups, and strong gravitational lensing.

Outside of astronomy, I swim and figure skate competitively, and love hiking and exploring nature.

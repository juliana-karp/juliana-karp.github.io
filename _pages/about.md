Hi! I'm an <a href='https://www.nsfgrfp.org/'>NSF Graduate Research Fellow</a> and <a href='https://seattle.arcsfoundation.org/'>ARCS Scholar</a> at the <a href='https://astro.washington.edu/'>University of Washington</a>, pursuing a PhD in Astronomy. My research interests lie at the intersection of galaxy formation and cosmology, using both near-field cosmology and strong gravitational lensing to observationally map galaxies' dark matter halos.

I received my B.S. in Astrophysics from <a href='https://astronomy.yale.edu/'>Yale University</a> in 2025. During my undergraduate degree, I researched anisotropic satellite galaxy quenching, galaxy groups, and strong gravitational lensing.

Outside of astronomy, I swim and figure skate competitively, and love hiking and exploring nature.
